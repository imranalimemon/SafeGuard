## Worker evaluation

`python -B backend/tools/violation_eval.py --predictions <split/predictions.json> --output <workers-output>` reran actual association/face logic using hash-verified detector outputs. Box-based ground truth is an exploratory geometric proxy that assumes complete annotation. Both splits yielded zero confirmed fully compliant workers; full compliance precision/recall is null. Most absent-mask truth is unknown without independent visible-face labels. Do not present proxy violation scores as validated safety accuracy.

## Defense wording

Say: "The implemented system supports reviewable live/upload PPE monitoring. Its observed detector and exploratory worker results are provisional because of split leakage and incomplete worker/face ground truth." Explain unknown mask states, person misses, position cooldown limitations and the human review workflow. Avoid claiming site-wide unique-worker compliance, guaranteed alerts, certified safety performance, or generalization from the leaked split.
